Lil-Sparrow-Tower-Defense

Lil' Sparrow Guy follows a sparrow named Gary who becomes trapped inside a building. To escape boredom, Gary discovers a gaming computer and begins playing a tower defense game, leading players through strategic defense gameplay with animated environments and progression-based mechanics.

Features
Strategic tower defense gameplay
Custom map and level design
Animated assets and interactive environments
Story-driven progression
